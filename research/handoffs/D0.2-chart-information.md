- compact donut values must describe the total, while keys and external legends identify slices;
- progress Micro must show its current percentage inside the radial mark;
- heatmap rows must use categorical row labels and larger rungs must expose a shared intensity scale;
- funnel stage geometry and labels must remain inside the measured SVG;
- grouped bar endpoint cells and direct line labels must remain inside the measured plot;
- the family matrix must report actual chart containment and readable-summary collisions at desktop
  and narrow widths, not only count DOM nodes.

## Implementation checkpoint

- Donut compact readouts now use the sum of all arc values and carry a visible `total` qualifier.
  Donut compact keys and larger external legends use slice labels, including the `Other` bucket,
  rather than the source series label.
- Progress radial Micro renders a centred `74%` value while larger rungs retain target-aware
  progress semantics.
- Heatmap y ticks are categorical series labels (`Maintenance`, `Inspection`); Canvas/Stage
  expose a five-step intensity legend with Low/High bounds and shared neutral swatches.
- Funnel renderer places the stage rectangle at its actual `x`/`y` and centres vertical labels;
  grouped bars clamp endpoint cells to the finite plot; direct labels anchor inward at the final
  point.
- Family-matrix metadata now names donut parts, funnel stages, heatmap rows, and metric values.
- The browser checker measures all visible SVG geometry against its card and reports SVG text
  collisions with the disclosure summary at both desktop and narrow viewports. Shape-only cards
  intentionally carry their readable legend outside the SVG; their HTML semantics remain covered
  by the static matrix assertions.
