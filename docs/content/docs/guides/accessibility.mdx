## Required names

Both `Chart` and `AutoChart` require `title`. Add `description` when the title alone cannot explain
the comparison or domain.

## Graphics semantics

The SVG uses `role="graphics-document"`, not `role="img"`. `role="img"` makes descendants
presentational and erases their structure from the accessibility tree.

## Data table

When the plan includes a table, ShiftCharts renders a real HTML `<table>` inside the figure's `<figcaption>`,
outside the SVG namespace. The table is part of the responsive ladder, not a bolted-on duplicate.

## Redundant identity

The final system uses hue, dash, and point shape together. Hue carries at most six series; series
seven and beyond must reuse hue and rely more strongly on form. The current A1-A6 theme supplies the
six verified series hues, and B1-B3 complete the delivered form/control surface for the line/area
renderer.

## Honest absence

If a rung cannot support a y-axis, label, or legend, the element is not rendered. Hidden content
still consumes accessibility attention and implies information that is not available visually.

Automated checks are necessary but insufficient. Inspect the browser accessibility tree and confirm
the SVG's title, description, and descendants are present.
